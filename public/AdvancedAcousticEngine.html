Advanced Acoustic Defense Engine

A Multi-Layered Architecture for Countering Modern Battlefield Threats

Executive Summary

The modern battlespace is characterized by an increase in low-observable aerial threats and sophisticated electronic and acoustic countermeasures. Traditional surveillance systems struggle to operate effectively in these complex, contested environments. The Advanced Acoustic Defense Engine (AADE) is a next-generation, multi-layered sensing platform designed to overcome these challenges. By integrating physically resilient hardware with intelligent, AI-driven software, the AADE provides robust, high-fidelity threat detection and classification. It reliably identifies the unique "machine fingerprints" of hostile assets, even amidst loud, deceptive jamming, delivering a decisive operational advantage in perimeter, mobile, and airborne defense scenarios.

The Evolving Battlefield

The modern battlefield is increasingly defined by stealth and deception. Adversaries employ advanced aerial threats designed to be quiet and hard to detect, while also using active countermeasures like loud sirens to confuse traditional sensors. This creates a complex acoustic environment where threats can easily hide, rendering conventional surveillance methods unreliable.

Our Solution: The Acoustic Defense Engine

The Acoustic Defense Engine is a smart listening system designed to overcome these challenges. It's not just a single device, but a network of advanced sensors and intelligent software working together. The core principle is "Optimized Listening," where every step of the process, from sound capture to analysis, is enhanced to pull faint threat sounds out of a noisy background.

How It Works: The Intelligent Advantage

The system's success comes from a multi-stage intelligent process that mimics expert human listening, but on a massive, computerized scale.

1. Resilience to Loud Noise & Deception

Threats like the Geran-2 drone use loud engines or sirens to overwhelm sensors. Our physical sensors are built with customized High Acoustic Overload Point (AOP) components, allowing them to listen without being deafened or saturated. They can cleanly capture both the loud jamming noise and the much fainter, critical sounds of the threat itself without data loss from clipping.

2. Hybrid Analysis for Acoustic Overload

When the system detects a high-decibel, broadband noise source like a siren, it enters a hybrid analysis mode. The initial physics-based filters dynamically adapt, treating the siren as a dominant but predictable signal. This allows the AI/ML models to focus their processing power on the residual acoustic data, effectively "listening through" the jamming to find the underlying, repeating patterns of the threat's propulsion system—its machine fingerprint.

3. Identifying the "Machine Fingerprint"

Our advanced software doesn't just listen for loudness; it listens for character. Our core algorithms, built on models such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and LSTMs, are trained using data that is extracted, mixed, and enhanced by our proprietary Acoustic AI framework. This framework generates a multitude of realistic environmental conditions and adversarial acoustic attack scenarios. This robust training allows the software to identify the unique, repeating spectrographic patterns—the 'machine fingerprint'—of specific threats, enabling it to lock onto the true target and ignore distracting background noise or deliberate jamming.

Scenario 01

Wide-Area Perimeter Defense

A layered perimeter defence deployment where high-gain Guardian nodes are cued by a wide network of low-cost mast sensors, providing early warning and directional inference across a 4.5 km radius hemisphere.

  • Distributed acoustic mast grid provides broad coverage
  • High-gain Guardian nodes triangulate threat bearing
  • Cued alerting reduces false positive rate to <0.1%
  • Targets: high-altitude UAVs, loitering munitions, artillery
Scenario 02

Coordinated Mobile Defense

A vehicle convoy shares real-time threat data via a mesh network link, enabling a coordinated response to an ambushing ground or aerial threat without requiring GCS communication.

  • Vehicle-mounted Guardian units form a rolling acoustic mesh
  • Peer-to-peer data fusion reduces response latency by 60%
  • 360° protective bubble per vehicle, 1.5 km radius
  • Targets: loitering munitions, enemy vehicles, attack helicopters
Scenario 03

Defeating Acoustic Jamming

The system isolates a drone's unique "machine fingerprint" from its loud siren countermeasure. Hybrid analysis mode dynamically adapts filters to treat the siren as a predictable broadband signal, allowing the AI to focus on the residual acoustic data from the propulsion system.

  • High AOP sensors capture full dynamic range without clipping
  • CNN/LSTM models identify spectrographic propulsion patterns
  • Effective against Geran-2 class siren-equipped threats
  • Lock maintained even at 130 dB SPL jamming level
Scenario 04

Combined Arms Defense

A heterogeneous network of Perimeter, Mobile, and Airborne Guardian platforms creates a complete, layered defensive shield against simultaneous multi-axis air and ground threats with no coverage gaps.

  • Perimeter nodes handle wide-area early warning
  • Mobile units protect high-value convoy assets
  • Airborne Guardian provides over-the-horizon cueing
  • Centralised fusion engine correlates all threat tracks
Scenario 05

Over-the-Horizon Swarm Detection

The Airborne Guardian, mounted on a fixed-wing ISR platform, detects an inbound drone swarm at long range (>10 km) via its forward-looking 60° acoustic cone, providing critical advance warning to all friendly assets in the area.

  • Detection range: >10 km (forward-looking)
  • 60° forward acoustic cone coverage
  • Swarm track count: up to 48 simultaneous targets
  • Alert-to-engagement timeline: <8 seconds from first detection

Platform Benefits & Operational Impact

The Acoustic Defense Engine's networked architecture is tailored to provide specific advantages across a range of operational platforms, creating a layered and resilient defense.

Perimeter Guardian

Fixed, high-sensitivity parabolic sensors are paired with a wide network of low-cost mast sensors. The mast sensors provide initial directional inference and early warning, cueing the high-gain sensors for precise classification at extreme ranges.

  • Impact: Creates a cost-effective, two-tiered defensive shield with no gaps. Wide-area cueing reduces the search sector for high-gain assets.
  • Detects: High-altitude drones, loitering munitions, incoming artillery.

Mobile Guardian

Vehicle-mounted 360° sensors provide a protective bubble for troops on the move.

  • Impact: Enables rapid, coordinated response by sharing real-time threat data across the entire unit.
  • Detects: Loitering munitions, enemy vehicles (tanks, trucks), attack helicopters.

Airborne Guardian

Sensors integrated into friendly drones provide a stealthy, forward-sensing "tripwire."

  • Impact: Delivers over-the-horizon situational awareness, cueing other assets long before they are in range.
  • Detects: Drone swarms, enemy air defenses, vehicle columns, fast-moving jets.
