The Sensor Fusion Ecosystem

Skhy isn't just hardware. It's an intelligent mesh where every sensor talks to the others. Our Fusion Engine correlates data across modalities to eliminate false positives and track threats with pinpoint precision.

1. Multi-Modal Sensing

Acoustic arrays listen, RF scanners detect signals, and cameras watch. Data is ingested from Skhy hardware (A7, A16) and third-party sensors simultaneously.

2. AI Fusion Engine

The core brain correlates inputs. A sound without a visual might be a false alarm; combined, it's a confirmed track. This fusion reduces nuisance alarms by 87%.

3. Actionable Response

Operators see a unified track on a 3D map, not raw data. The system calculates hazard radii and auto-tasks cameras to the threat location.
