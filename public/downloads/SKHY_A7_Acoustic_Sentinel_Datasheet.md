## Overview
The SKHY-A7 is a compact acoustic sensor designed for autonomous aerial and ground-based detection. It integrates a 7-MEMS microphone array within a precision-machined aluminum housing, providing high sensitivity and directional accuracy in a lightweight form factor.

## Key Features
- **Passive Detection**: Completely silent operation; does not emit signals, making it undetectable to targets.
- **Edge Compute Ready**: Interfaces directly with edge AI computers (like NVIDIA Jetson) for real-time classification.
- **Robust Build**: 85mm aluminum shielding reduces EMI and protects sensitive components.
- **Multi-Platform**: Ideal for mid-sized drones and tactical ground vehicles.
