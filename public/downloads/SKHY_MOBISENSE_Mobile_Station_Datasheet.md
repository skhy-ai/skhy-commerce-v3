## Overview
The SKHY-MOBISENSE is a turnkey, rapidly deployable solution for perimeter defense and situational awareness. It correlates acoustic signatures with radio frequency (RF) intelligence to virtually eliminate false positives and provide a comprehensive threat picture.

## Key Features
- **Sensor Fusion**: Correlates acoustic and RF signals for validated threat detection.
- **Multi-Band RF**: Monitors frequencies from 400MHz to 8GHz, covering standard drone and communication links.
- **Stealth Operation**: Passive sensors do not emit signals, maintaining operational security.
- **Rugged Deployment**: Designed for quick setup in the field with integrated processing via a SKHY Light Edge AI device.
