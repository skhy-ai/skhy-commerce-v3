## Overview
AeroGuard series protective cones are engineered for airborne applications where aerodynamic efficiency and physical protection are paramount. Manufactured from ultra-lightweight carbon fiber, these cones minimize drag on fixed-wing drones while maintaining acoustic transparency.

## Key Features
- **Aerodynamic Optimization**: Reduces wind noise and drag for improved flight endurance and sensor performance.
- **Acoustic Transparency**: Specialized mesh allows sound waves to penetrate without distortion.
- **Ultra-Lightweight**: Minimal impact on drone payload capacity.
- **Environmental Protection**: Shields sensors from moisture, dust, and debris during flight.
