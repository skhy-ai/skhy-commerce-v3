## Overview
The SKHY Surveyor is a high-endurance, multi-modal reconnaissance platform. By combining the efficiency of a fixed-wing aircraft with the vertical launch capabilities of a multi-rotor, it provides persistent surveillance in remote or contested areas without requiring infrastructure.

## Intelligence Variants

### Seeker
- **Payload**: Standard Visual + 8-Mic Acoustic Array
- **Role**: Stealthy detection and tracking of engine-driven threats.

### Conjurer
- **Payload**: Thermal Imaging + Advanced RF Signal Intelligence
- **Role**: Multi-spectral nighttime ISR and spectrum monitoring.

### Wizard
- **Payload**: Full Fusion Suite + SKHY Light Edge AI Node
- **Role**: Autonomous edge classification and real-time sensor fusion.

## Key Features
- **Acoustic Intelligence**: Detects and localizes enemy drones, helicopters, and jets at extreme ranges.
- **Jam-Resilient Links**: Integrated with SKHY MANET and AJGPS systems.
