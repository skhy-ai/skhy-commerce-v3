## Overview
The SKHY-A16 is a high-sensitivity acoustic array designed for long-range threat detection and high-value asset protection. By utilizing a 16-MEMS array and a larger acoustic aperture, it captures lower frequency signatures and provides extended warning times for high-speed aircraft.

## Key Features
- **Extended Horizon**: Detects jet take-offs from up to 250km away, providing strategic early warning.
- **Superior Accuracy**: 16-channel input enables advanced beamforming and direction-of-arrival (DoA) accuracy.
- **Heavy-Duty Shielding**: Optimized for stationary ground installations or heavy-lift drone platforms.
- **Spectrum Coverage**: Enhanced sensitivity to low-frequency engine signatures.
