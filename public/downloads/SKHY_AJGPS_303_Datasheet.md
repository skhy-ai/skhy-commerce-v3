## Overview
The AJGPS 303 is a premium multi-band anti-interference system specifically designed for high-precision navigation. It protects the critical GPS L2 and L5 bands alongside L1, ensuring that RTK and high-accuracy positioning remains functional even under targeted jamming.

## Key Features
- **Triple-Band GPS Protection**: Simultaneously protects L1, L2, and L5 frequencies.
- **Enhanced Suppression**: ≥105dB for single broadband interference.
- **Precision Navigation**: Enables continued use of dual/triple-frequency receivers in contested RF environments.
- **Robust Enclosure**: Designed for high-vibration and high-impact environments.
