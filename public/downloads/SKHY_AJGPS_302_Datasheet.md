## Overview
The AJGPS 302 extends the capabilities of the 300-series by adding support for the GLONASS G1 frequency. It is designed for platforms requiring robust navigation across all four major global navigation satellite systems in presence of high-power interference.

## Key Features
- **Quad-Constellation Protection**: Comprehensive coverage for GPS, BeiDou, Galileo, and GLONASS.
- **High Suppression Ratio**: ≥100dB for single broadband interference.
- **Anti-Deception**: Advanced algorithms to prevent signal hijacking.
- **Industrial Grade**: Designed for reliable operation in extreme temperatures (-40℃ to 70℃).
