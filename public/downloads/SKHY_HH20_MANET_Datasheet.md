## Overview
The HH20 is a rugged, handheld MANET radio designed for ground-based personnel and mobile teams. It provides dual-band connectivity to the mesh network, allowing field operators to receive high-definition video feeds and telemetry from overhead drones.

## Key Features
- **Tactical Mobility**: Compact, handheld form factor for foot soldiers and vehicle teams.
- **Dual-Band Connectivity**: Automatic frequency selection for optimal signal strength in urban or rural areas.
- **Real-Time Data**: Directly stream drone video and mission data to handheld devices.
- **Rugged Durability**: IP-rated enclosure for reliable operation in rain, dust, and extreme heat.
