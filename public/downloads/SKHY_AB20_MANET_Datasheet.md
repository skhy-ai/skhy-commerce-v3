## Overview
The AB20 is a versatile, mid-range MANET radio designed for Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR) missions. Featuring integrated active cooling, it can handle high-duty cycles and high-bandwidth data transmission from drone payloads over significant distances.

## Key Features
- **Active Cooling**: Integrated fan ensures consistent performance during prolonged high-throughput transmission.
- **Optimized SWaP**: Excellent balance of range, weight, and power for mid-sized UAV platforms.
- **Multi-Node Mesh**: Seamlessly integrates into larger SKHY MANET networks.
- **Robust Links**: Advanced modulation and error correction for stable video transmission.
