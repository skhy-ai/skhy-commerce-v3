1. The Build Phase

Structural & Aerodynamic Foundations

Before a mission begins, the physical capabilities of the asset must be strictly defined. The Mission Architect allows operators to configure payload mass, cruise airspeed, and thrust dynamics to calculate real-world performance metrics across three platform types: Vertical Lift (multirotor), Fixed-Wing, and Ground Rover (UGV).

  • Payload & Range Logic

    Real-time Breguet Range Formula calculations map endurance against payload constraints for all platform types.

  • Aerodynamic Metrics

    Visualizing glide ratios (L/D), cruise thrust, and hovering thrust loads for fixed-wing and multi-rotor assets with a live 3D model.

  • Multi-Platform Support

    Switch seamlessly between Vertical Lift, Fixed-Wing, and Rover (UGV) to compare configurations before committing to hardware.

2. Transmission Resiliency

Robust Multi-Band Communications

Securing the data link is paramount. The Transmission domain models Signal-to-Interference Ratios (SIR) and validates the network architecture between the Ground Control Station (GCS), the drone, and aerial relays.

  • Live Spectrum Analysis

    Evaluate uplink and downlink signal power against adversarial jammer interference dynamically across the operational range.

  • Signal Relay Architecture

    Simulate the deployment of high-altitude fixed-wing relays to bypass line-of-sight constraints and EW kill zones.

3. Tactical Positioning & Anti-Jamming

Navigating contested environments requires predictive routing and hardened reception. The Mission Architect simulates GNSS denial and validates Controlled Reception Pattern Antenna (CRPA) performance.

Terrain & Threat Avoidance

Deploy assets onto a real-world map to visualize Electronic Warfare (EW) Kill Zones. The system automatically generates optimized, EW-resilient flight corridors that avoid known jammer threats while maintaining required standoff distances.

Multi-Constellation CRPA Validation

Configure anti-jamming hardware arrays (e.g., SKHY AJGPS 601) to simulate null-steering against multiple interference sources. Operators can visualize null generation and aggregate GNSS Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) in real-time under attack conditions.

4. System Architecture

Mission Wiring & Hardware Topology

The Architecture phase visualizes every hardware component and its interconnections as an interactive node-based wiring diagram. Operators confirm that power rails, data buses, RF/antenna links, and control/PWM pathways are correctly configured before manufacturing.

  • Component-Level Connectivity

    Each node represents a physical component (Battery, Flight Controller, AJGPS, ESC, Payload) with real-world pin-level wiring connections displayed visually.

  • Live Power Budget

    Real-time aggregate current draw, bus power load, core load, and mission load are tracked in the top HUD to prevent overspecification or thermal overruns.

  • Multi-Bus Signal Routing

    Separate colour-coded buses for Power Rail, Data/UART, Control/PWM, RF/Antenna, Video Downlink, and Ground ensure electrical integrity is verified before build.
