This agreement establishes the formal partnership between SKHY AI SOLUTIONS and ACASOM for the distribution, marketing, and sale of ACASOM-manufactured RF amplifiers, antennas, and jamming modules through the SKHY AI Marketplace.

## 2. PRICING & MSRP PROTECTION

2.2. **Base Prices**: The current prices listed in the SKHY AI Marketplace under Manufacturer "ACASOM" are hereby verified and locked as the baseline for this agreement.
2.3. **Discounts**: Any promotional discounting or volume-based pricing must be mutually agreed upon in writing 15 days prior to implementation.

## 3. PRODUCT INTEGRITY & BRANDING
3.1. **Accurate Representation**: The Platform will ensure all ACASOM products include accurate technical specifications, high-resolution imagery, and proper branding.

5.2. The Platform is granted a non-exclusive license to use Supplier branding for marketplace marketing purposes only.
