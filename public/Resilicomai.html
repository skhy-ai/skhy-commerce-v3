Executive Summary

The modern electromagnetic environment is increasingly congested and contested. State and non-state actors deploy sophisticated electronic warfare (EW) tactics like RF jamming and GNSS spoofing, threatening the integrity of critical communication and navigation systems. Conventional antenna technologies with static radiation patterns are fundamentally ill-equipped to counter these dynamic, multi-vector threats. This whitepaper introduces a paradigm shift: the SKHY Resilicom AI Box. By integrating advanced reconfigurable hardware with a reinforcement learning core, our technology provides autonomous, real-time electronic protection, ensuring persistent connectivity and positional accuracy for assets across all operational domains.

The Challenge: A Contested Spectrum

From tactical operations on the ground to strategic satellite communications, reliance on the electromagnetic spectrum is absolute. However, this reliance is also a critical vulnerability. The proliferation of low-cost, high-power jammers and sophisticated spoofing systems can create "denied" zones, severing command and control (C2), blinding ISR assets, and compromising PNT (Positioning, Navigation, and Timing) data. Legacy systems, which often require manual intervention or rely on slow-to-adapt filtering, cannot keep pace with threats that change frequency, location, and waveform in seconds. A new class of intelligent, autonomous, and adaptive technology is required to achieve spectrum dominance.
