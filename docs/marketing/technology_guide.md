## 1. Core Technology: "Vidur AI OS"

At the heart of SKHY AI is **Why Vidur AI?**
We move defense from "Point Solutions" (isolated, single-purpose tools) to a **"Platform OS"** (unified, extensible, and intelligent).

### Key Technologies

*   **Vidur AI OS (Operating System)**
    *   **Unified Intelligence**: A single coordination layer that ingests data from acoustic sensors, RF monitors, and visual cameras.
    *   **Sovereign & Secure**: Designed to run on **air-gapped** infrastructure, eliminating reliance on foreign clouds (US/China).
    *   **Red-Black Separation**: Strict isolation between standard operational data ("Black" Zone) and classified intelligence ("Red" Zone).
    *   **Sensor Fusion**: Merges multiple data streams (e.g., sound + radio waves) to eliminate 87% of false positives.

*   **Edge Compute (Fusion Nodes)**
    *   **High Performance**: Custom hardware nodes capable of **67 TOPS** (Trillions of Operations Per Second).
    *   **Local AI**: All AI processing happens *on the device* or *on premises*, ensuring zero latency and 100% data sovereignty.
    *   **Hardware Guard**: Physical FPGA-based monitoring to detect tampering or hardware anomalies.
    *   **Deployment**: Runs on ruggedized hardware (Fusion Node) for harsh environments.

*   **Signal Analysis (SDR Stack)**
    *   **Software Defined Radio (SDR)**: Universal ingest of radio frequencies for spectrum monitoring.
    *   **Anti-Jamming AI**: Detects and mitigates GPS jamming and RF interference in real-time.
    *   **MIMO Technology**: Multiple-Input Multiple-Output arrays for precise signal localization.

*   **Connectivity (MANET)**
    *   **Mobile Ad-hoc Networking**: Decentralized mesh networking (AB40, AB20 nodes) that works even when standard infrastructure (Cellular/Internet) fails.
    *   **Resilience**: Self-healing networks for drones, ground vehicles, and remote assets.

## 4. Strategic Use Cases & Case Studies

SKHY AI solves critical "blindness" and "fragility" problems across multiple sectors.

### Defense & National Security

#### **Case Study: APAC Sovereign Defense**
*   **Problem**: "The Sovereignty Trap"—Reliance on US/China clouds creates national security risks for ASEAN nations.
*   **Solution**: **Apricot / Openspace**. Building a localized Sovereign AI OS.
*   **Application**: Air-gapped AI that keeps intelligence data within national borders.
*   **Outcome**: No foreign kill-switches, full data control.

#### **Case Study: Drone Defense**
*   **Problem**: Signal blindness in high-density urban skylines makes radar ineffective against small drones.
*   **Solution**: **Urban Skyline Defense**. Using acoustic and visual fusion to detect drones where radar fails.
*   **Application**: Protecting critical infrastructure in smart cities.
*   **Outcome**: 99.9% detection rate in clutter-rich environments.

#### **Case Study: Electronic Warfare (EW)**
*   **Problem**: "Physical Tail Risk"—Assets are vulnerable to cheap GPS jamming and RF attacks.
*   **Solution**: **Anti-Jamming AI**.
*   **Application**: Protecting tanks, ships, and command posts from electronic fragility.
*   **Outcome**: Operational continuity during active jamming.

*   **Application**: Onboard AI that navigates using visual/inertial/RF cues when GPS is spoofed.

#### **Case Study: Supply Chain Resilience**

*   **Solution**: **Heritas**. Secure autonomous delivery for contested or remote zones.
*   **Application**: Resilient logistics tech that doesn't rely on constant cloud connectivity.

#### **Case Study: Infrastructure Protection**
